Why Clinical Coder Pro™ is Completely Air-Gapped & Impenetrable

Traditional medical software stores patient health information (PHI) in unencrypted local folders or exposes database ports across your clinic’s local network, leaving practices vulnerable to clearinghouse ransomware breaches. Our sovereign architecture eliminates this attack vector entirely through four military-grade defense rails:

🔒 Zero-Disk Ephemeral Memory (RAM Only)

Clinical notes are held strictly in temporary RAM during scrubbing and are cryptographically sanitized the second a claim is compiled. Zero patient records ever persist on your computer's hard drive.

🛡️ Ghost-Mode (Zero Open Ports)

The engine operates with zero inbound listening ports. Hackers or automated malware scanning your clinic’s internal Wi-Fi or LAN cannot detect or establish a connection to our module.

🔑 Hardware-Bound Cryptographic Vault

Internal payer rule sets and ICD/CPT crosswalks are secured with AES-256-GCM encryption tied directly to your physical PC’s TPM chip. Even if a hard drive were physically stolen, code cannot be booted elsewhere.

⚡ Anti-Tamper Cryptographic Code Signing

Every quarterly rule update is signed with Vance & Calder’s private Ed25519 cryptographic key. The engine validates integrity on startup and halts execution if altered by malware or external tampering.
