VANCE & CALDER FORTIFIED VAULT™ ARCHITECTURE

Why Vance & Calder Clinical Coder Pro™ is Impenetrable:

Traditional medical software stores patient health information (PHI) in unencrypted local folders or exposes database ports to the clinic's local area network, making them easy targets for ransomware syndicates. Our architecture eliminates this attack vector entirely through four military-grade defense rails:

🔒 Zero-Disk Ephemeral Memory

Clinical notes are held strictly in temporary RAM during processing and cryptographically sanitized from memory the moment a claim is verified. No patient records ever sit on the hard drive.

🛡️ Ghost-Mode (Zero Open Ports)

The program runs with zero inbound listening ports. Hackers or automated malware scanning the clinic's internal network cannot detect or establish a connection to our module.

🔑 AES-256 Hardware-Bound Vault

Internal logic and code databases are locked with AES-256-GCM encryption tied to the host machine's hardware TPM chip. Even if a hard drive is physically stolen, it cannot be read elsewhere.

⚡ Anti-Tamper Code Signing

Every quarterly update is signed with Vance & Calder’s private Ed25519 cryptographic key. The engine verifies integrity on boot and refuses execution if altered by malware.

The Pitch to Clinic Owners: "Even if your clinic suffered a catastrophic full-network ransomware attack, the Vance & Calder engine holds zero persistent data on disk to ransom or steal. Your revenue cycle is completely insulated."
